# Audio credits / 音效来源

Every sound effect in **Operation Red Sand** is a real recording taken from free,
public-domain (CC0 1.0) libraries — no procedural synthesis is used for gameplay
sound any more. CC0 does not require attribution, but the sources are listed here
so the audio set stays traceable and can be rebuilt or replaced.

All files served from `public/assets/audio/<game-sound-name>/` are generated by
`assets-src/sfx/build.mjs` from the original recordings listed below.

Firearms are mastered from the raw captures with one neutral chain: 25 Hz high-pass, a lookahead
limiter, and an exponential tail fade - no synthetic low-end layer and no EQ curve. Each shot is
trimmed to its own onset, its tail ends where the capture falls 32 dB below the shot peak, and the
150 ms impact body of every variant of a weapon is normalised to the same level so random variants
do not jump in the mix. The level is verified on the decoded mp3 (peak stays under -1 dBFS).
`node tools/build-gun-review.mjs` regenerates `gun-review.html`, a self-contained audition sheet
for all 23 gunshot variants.

## Rebuilding

The source recordings and the ffmpeg pipeline live in `assets-src/sfx/`
(git-ignored, not shipped). The game only needs the generated files:

```
public/assets/audio/<name>/01.mp3 …
public/assets/audio/manifest.json
src/core/audioSamples.ts   (generated manifest the engine loads)
```

Run `node assets-src/sfx/build.mjs` to re-cut, re-normalise and re-encode every
sound after editing the `PLAN` table (source file, slice window, gain).
